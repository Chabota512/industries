---
name: Walk-in visit verification
description: Product constraints for using letters and company details to plan in-person attachment visits.
---

The product helps the user prepare and travel to physical offices for industrial attachment applications in Zambia. The user's Google Drive letters are the source for company names, and letters uploaded through the app should be saved to the selected Drive folder. The app needs owner-only access to read Drive letters and create new files there. A company name in a Drive folder or filename is only a lead; it does not establish an office location, HR contact, receiving hours, or that walk-in applications are accepted. Keep office records separate from source letters and mark details unverified until confirmed. Only open Google Maps for a precise address or coordinates.

**Why:** The user specified that Drive letters supply company names and app uploads belong in Drive. A wrong office or unconfirmed walk-in policy can waste a real trip.

**How to apply:** Treat Drive letters as the company-name source. Import filenames only as unverified candidate names, allow the owner to preview letters, and save new uploads to Drive without replacing existing files. Do not infer office details or recommend travel until the destination is confirmed.
