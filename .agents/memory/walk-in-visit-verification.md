---
name: Walk-in visit verification
description: Product constraints for using letters and company details to plan in-person attachment visits.
---

The product helps the user prepare and travel to physical offices for industrial attachment applications in Zambia. A company name in a Drive folder or filename is only a lead; it does not establish an office location, HR contact, receiving hours, or that walk-in applications are accepted. Keep office records separate from source letters and mark details unverified until confirmed. Only open Google Maps for a precise address or coordinates.

**Why:** A wrong office or unconfirmed walk-in policy can waste a real trip, and prior office information was uncertain.

**How to apply:** Import filenames only as unverified candidate names. Do not infer office details or recommend travel until the destination is confirmed.
